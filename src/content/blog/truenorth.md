### TrueNorth AI-Powered Accessibility & Market Intelligence Platform

**Hackville Project — Ontario, Canada**

*   Built an AI-driven web platform using Astro, JavaScript, and TypeScript to bring visibility to underserved Canadian communities by highlighting accessibility and service gaps often missed by traditional market analysis.
*   Developed a cost-efficient Google Cloud setup with structured JSON data pipelines, scoped web scraping, and clear documentation, earning **1st place awards for Best Canadian Focus (Simple Ventures) and Best Use of Google Cloud**.
