### Breast Cancer Classification: J48 vs MLP

**Machine Learning & Data Analysis Project (Excel & Weka) — Oakville, ON**

*   Compared J48 Decision Tree (71.1% accuracy) and MLP (67.6%) on the UCI Breast Cancer dataset over 30 trials; recommended J48 based on Excel analysis and supporting research.
*   Performed data cleaning, validation, and visualization in Weka and Excel, effectively communicating model performance and insights to peers.
